# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- **Focus mode:** while a fishing pole is in your main hand, music, ambience, NPC dialog, emote sounds, pet sounds and error voice lines are off, and sound effects are at full volume, so the bobber's splash stands out. Sound in the background is on too. Everything goes back when the pole comes off.
- Your values are saved first and restored on logout, or at the next login after a crash. A setting you change yourself while fishing is left as you set it.
- `/fishio`, `/fishio on | off`, `/fishio quiet | loud`.
